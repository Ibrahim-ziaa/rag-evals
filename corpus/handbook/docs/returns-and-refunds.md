# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
You can return any appliance within 30 days of delivery if you are not satisfied, even if it has been installed and used. After 30 days we can only accept a return when the appliance is faulty and covered by the warranty.

## Condition and fees
The appliance must be clean, complete and free of damage beyond normal use. A restocking fee of 15 percent applies to appliances that have been installed. The restocking fee is waived when the appliance is unopened in its original packaging, or when the return is due to a fault.

## How to start a return
Request a return from the Orders page of your account or by contacting support. We issue a return authorisation number, which is valid for 10 days. Do not send an appliance back without a return authorisation number, because the warehouse will refuse the delivery.

## Collection
Halden arranges collection by the same carrier that delivered the appliance. Return collection costs 79 USD per appliance, deducted from the refund. Collection is free when the return is due to a fault or a delivery error.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Water filters and cleaning products once the seal is broken.
- Replacement parts that have been fitted.
- Appliances bought from the clearance outlet, which are sold as final sale.

## When the refund arrives
We inspect the appliance within 3 business days of it reaching our warehouse and then approve the refund. See the Billing and Payments page for how long an approved refund takes to reach your card or bank account.
