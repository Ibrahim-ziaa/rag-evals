# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery options
- Standard delivery: free on all major appliances. The carrier brings the appliance to your front door or the ground floor entrance of your building.
- Room of choice delivery: 49 USD. Two people carry the appliance to the room you choose, up to two flights of stairs, and unpack it.
- Delivery with installation: 149 USD. Includes room of choice delivery, connection to existing water and power, and a test cycle.

## Delivery times
Appliances that are in stock are delivered within 5 to 8 business days of the order being placed. Rural postcodes can take up to 12 business days. The carrier contacts you at least 48 hours before delivery to agree a 4 hour delivery window.

## Small items
Filters, replacement parts and accessories ship by parcel post. Parcel shipping costs 6 USD and is free for parcel orders over 50 USD. Parcels arrive within 2 to 4 business days.

## Inspecting your delivery
Check the packaging and the appliance before the driver leaves. If you see damage, write it on the delivery note and refuse the appliance, and we will send a replacement at no cost. Damage found after the driver has left must be reported within 48 hours with photographs.

## Where we deliver
We deliver to all 48 contiguous states. We do not currently deliver to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico or addresses outside the United States. We cannot deliver to a PO box.

## Missed delivery
If nobody is home during the agreed window, the carrier will reschedule once for free. A second missed delivery carries a 35 USD redelivery charge.
