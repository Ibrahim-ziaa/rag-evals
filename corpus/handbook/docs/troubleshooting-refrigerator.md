# Refrigerator Troubleshooting

## The refrigerator is not cold enough
Check that the door closes fully and the gasket is clean. Make sure the vents at the back of the fresh food compartment are not blocked by food. After loading a lot of warm food, the refrigerator can take up to 24 hours to return to the set temperature.

## Frost is building up in the freezer
Frost forms when warm, moist air enters, which usually means the door was left ajar or the gasket is not sealing. Close a sheet of paper in the door: if it slides out easily, the gasket needs cleaning or replacing. R-series freezers defrost automatically and should never need manual defrosting.

## Water under the crisper drawers
The defrost drain at the back of the compartment is blocked. Clear it with the small plastic plunger supplied, or with warm water and a pipe cleaner.

## The refrigerator is noisy
A low hum, occasional clicking and a gurgling sound are normal operating noises. Rattling usually means the refrigerator is touching a wall or that bottles are touching each other. A loud buzzing that lasts more than 10 minutes on ice maker models means the water line is closed or frozen.

## The ice maker has stopped making ice
Check that the ice maker is switched on in the settings menu and that the freezer is at minus 15 degrees Celsius or colder. Replace the water filter if it is more than 6 months old, because a clogged filter reduces water flow to the ice maker.

## The door alarm keeps sounding
The alarm sounds when a door has been open for more than 2 minutes. If it sounds with the doors closed, check that nothing is stopping the door from sealing and that the refrigerator is tilted slightly backwards so the doors swing shut.
