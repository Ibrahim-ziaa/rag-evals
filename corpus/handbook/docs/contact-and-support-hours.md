# Contacting Halden Support

## Phone
Call 1-800-555-0117, Monday to Friday from 8 am to 8 pm and Saturday from 9 am to 5 pm, Eastern Time. Phone support is closed on Sundays and on public holidays. The average wait is shortest before 10 am.

## Live chat
Live chat is available from the Help button on every page of halden.example, every day from 7 am to 10 pm Eastern Time. Outside these hours the chat assistant can answer common questions and create a ticket for the team.

## Email
Email support@halden.example. We reply to emails within 1 business day. Include your order number or serial number so that we do not have to ask for it.

## What to have ready
- The model number and serial number from the rating plate.
- Your order number, if the question is about a delivery or a refund.
- Any error code shown on the display.
- A photograph or short video if the problem is visible or audible.

## Complaints
If you are unhappy with how a case was handled, ask for it to be escalated to the Customer Relations team. Customer Relations responds to every escalated case within 3 business days, and a named case manager stays with your case until it is closed.

## Accessibility
Customers who are deaf or hard of hearing can reach us through the relay service on 711. Manuals are available in large print and as screen reader friendly PDF files on request.
