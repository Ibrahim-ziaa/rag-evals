# Price Match and Promotions

## Price match
If you find the same model at a lower price from an authorised US retailer, we match the price before you buy. The competing offer must be for a new appliance, in stock and available for delivery to your postcode. Send a link to the offer through live chat and we reply with a one time discount code within 2 hours during chat opening times.

## What we do not match
We do not match marketplace sellers, auction sites, membership only prices, clearance or open box stock, or prices that depend on buying another product.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order. Codes cannot be applied after the order has been placed. Discount codes do not apply to Halden Care, delivery services or installation.

## Bundle discount
Buying three or more major appliances in a single order earns a 10 percent bundle discount on the whole order. If you return one appliance from a bundle and fewer than three remain, the bundle discount is removed from the refund.

## Trade and volume pricing
Builders, landlords and property managers ordering five or more appliances a year can apply for a trade account, which gives fixed volume pricing and a dedicated account manager. Apply at halden.example/trade.

## Student, military and first responder offer
Verified students, serving military, veterans and first responders receive 5 percent off major appliances. Verification is completed online at checkout and the discount is applied straight away.
