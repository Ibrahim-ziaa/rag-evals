# Dryer Troubleshooting

## Clothes take too long to dry
Clean the lint filter, and on heat pump models rinse the condenser filter as well. Make sure the laundry was spun at 1,200 rpm or faster in the washing machine. Do not overload the drum: a full load should leave a hand width of space at the top.

## The dryer stops after a few minutes
The water container is full or the sensor thinks the load is already dry. Empty the water container at the top left after every cycle. Very small loads may not touch the moisture sensors, so use a timed programme for one or two items.

## The drum does not turn
Check that the door is pushed firmly shut until it clicks. If the drum light comes on and you can hear the motor but the drum is still, the drive belt has probably failed and a technician visit is needed.

## Clothes come out creased
Remove laundry as soon as the programme ends, or select the Anti Crease option, which turns the drum at intervals for up to 2 hours. Dry shirts on the Shirts programme with no more than five in a load.

## Water on the floor
Check that the water container is seated properly and that the door seal is free of lint. If the dryer drains through a hose, make sure the hose is not kinked or pushed too far into the drain.

## Burning smell
Stop the dryer, unplug it and clean the lint filter and the filter housing. If the smell returns on the next cycle, stop using the dryer and contact support.
