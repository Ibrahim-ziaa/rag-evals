# Financing and Installment Plans

## Pay in installments
Orders between 300 USD and 5,000 USD can be split into monthly installments at checkout. Installment plans are provided by our lending partner, Northfield Credit, and are subject to a credit check.

## Plan lengths and interest
- 6 month plan: 0 percent interest.
- 12 month plan: 0 percent interest on orders over 1,000 USD, otherwise 7.9 percent APR.
- 24 month plan: 9.9 percent APR.

There is no fee for setting up a plan and no penalty for paying it off early.

## Late payments
A late installment incurs a 15 USD late fee after a grace period of 10 days. Three missed installments in a row cancel the promotional interest rate, and the remaining balance then accrues interest at 19.9 percent APR.

## Returns on financed orders
If you return an appliance bought on an installment plan, the refund goes to Northfield Credit and your plan balance is reduced or closed. Installments you have already paid are refunded to your bank account within 10 business days.

## Questions about your plan
Halden support cannot see or change your installment schedule. For statements, payment dates and changes to your bank details, contact Northfield Credit directly through the link in your plan confirmation email.
