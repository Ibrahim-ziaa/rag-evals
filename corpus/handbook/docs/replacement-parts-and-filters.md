# Replacement Parts and Filters

## Ordering parts
Genuine Halden parts are sold at halden.example/parts. Search by the model number on the rating plate to see only the parts that fit your appliance. Parts are stocked for at least 10 years after a model is discontinued.

## Water filters
R-series refrigerators use the HF-2 water filter, which costs 39 USD. Replace it every 6 months or after 750 litres, whichever comes first. A filter subscription delivers a new filter every 6 months at a 15 percent discount and can be paused or cancelled at any time.

## Fitting parts yourself
Racks, baskets, filters, shelves and door gaskets can be fitted without tools and do not affect your warranty. Parts that carry mains voltage or refrigerant, such as pumps, heating elements and compressors, must be fitted by a Halden technician. A fault caused by a self fitted electrical part is not covered by the warranty.

## Parts warranty
Replacement parts carry a 12 month warranty from the purchase date. Parts fitted by a Halden technician during a paid repair carry the same 12 month warranty, and it also covers the labour.
