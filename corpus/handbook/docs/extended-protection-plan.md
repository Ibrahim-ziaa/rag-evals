# Halden Care Extended Protection Plan

## What the plan adds
Halden Care extends the standard warranty by 3 years, for a total of 5 years of cover from the delivery date. It also adds accidental damage cover, which the standard warranty does not include, and one free maintenance visit per year.

## Price
Halden Care costs 129 USD for a dishwasher or dryer, 149 USD for a washing machine and 179 USD for a refrigerator. The price is a single payment and there are no monthly fees.

## When you can buy it
You can add Halden Care at checkout or at any time within 60 days of delivery. After 60 days the plan can only be added following a paid inspection that confirms the appliance is in working order.

## Cancelling the plan
You can cancel Halden Care within 30 days of buying it for a full refund, provided no claim has been made. After 30 days the refund is prorated by the number of full months remaining on the plan.

## Claim limits
There is no limit on the number of repair claims. Accidental damage claims are limited to two per appliance over the life of the plan. If we replace the appliance under the plan, the plan ends and does not carry over to the replacement.
