# Installing Your Dishwasher

## Before you start
A built in Halden dishwasher needs an opening 600 mm wide, at least 820 mm high and 570 mm deep. You need a cold water supply with a shut off valve, a drain connection and an earthed power socket within 1.5 metres. Do not plug the dishwasher into an extension lead.

## Water connection
Connect the inlet hose to a cold water supply only. The water pressure must be between 0.5 and 10 bar. Tighten the hose by hand and then a further quarter turn with pliers. The inlet hose includes an automatic leak stop valve, so it must not be cut or extended.

## Drain connection
The drain hose must rise to at least 400 mm above the floor before it joins the waste pipe. This high loop stops dirty water from the sink flowing back into the dishwasher. The drain hose can be extended to a maximum length of 4 metres using the Halden extension kit.

## Levelling and fixing
Adjust the front feet until the dishwasher is level from side to side and from front to back. Fix the dishwasher to the underside of the worktop with the two brackets supplied. On stone worktops, use the side fixing holes instead.

## First run
Remove the transport clips from the racks. Fill the salt container if your water is hard, add rinse aid, and run the Intensive programme empty before washing dishes for the first time. Check under the dishwasher for leaks during this first cycle.
