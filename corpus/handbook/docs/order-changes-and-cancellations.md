# Order Changes and Cancellations

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is handed to the carrier. Once the order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled. Refuse the delivery or follow the returns process instead, and the normal return conditions apply.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed up to 24 hours before the scheduled delivery window. Changes are made from the Orders page or by phone. A change to an address in a different state may add up to 5 business days to the delivery time.

## Changing the model or colour
If you want a different model or colour, contact support before dispatch and we will swap the item on the same order. Any price difference is charged or refunded to the original payment method.

## Back orders
When a model is out of stock the product page shows an estimated dispatch date. Your card is not charged for a back ordered item until it is dispatched. If the estimated date moves by more than 14 days, we email you and you can cancel for a full refund.

## Price adjustments
If the price of the appliance you bought drops on halden.example within 14 days of your order date, contact support and we will refund the difference. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance outlet prices or to bundle discounts.
