# Washing Machine Troubleshooting

## The machine shakes or moves during the spin
Check that all four transit bolts were removed at installation. Make sure the machine is level and the lock nuts on the feet are tight. A single heavy item such as a bath mat cannot be balanced, so add a few towels to the load.

## The door will not open
The door stays locked for up to 2 minutes after the programme ends. It also stays locked while there is water in the drum. If the machine has lost power with water inside, drain it through the emergency drain hose behind the filter flap at the bottom right, then pull the red emergency release tab.

## Clothes are too wet after the cycle
The machine reduces the spin speed when the load is unbalanced or when there is too much foam. Use less detergent, wash a larger mixed load and check that the drain pump filter is clean.

## The machine does not fill with water
Check that the tap is fully open and the inlet hose is not kinked. Unscrew the inlet hose from the machine and clean the small mesh filter inside the connection.

## Musty smell or mould on the door seal
Leave the door and the detergent drawer open between washes so the drum can dry. Wipe the folds of the door seal each week. Run the Drum Clean programme at 90 degrees once a month with no laundry.

## Detergent is left in the drawer
Low water pressure or a clogged drawer is the usual cause. Pull the drawer out fully, press the release tab and rinse it under warm water.
