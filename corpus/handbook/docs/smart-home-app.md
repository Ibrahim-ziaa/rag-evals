# Halden Home App and Connected Appliances

## What the app does
The Halden Home app connects to appliances with a Wi-Fi symbol on the control panel. It sends a notification when a cycle ends, lets you start a programme remotely, shows energy and water use per cycle, and reminds you when maintenance is due. The app is free for iOS 16 or later and Android 11 or later.

## Connecting an appliance
Connected appliances use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only and cannot join a 5 GHz network. Hold the Remote button for 5 seconds until the Wi-Fi symbol flashes, then follow the pairing steps in the app. Stay within 3 metres of the appliance while pairing.

## Remote start
For safety, remote start must be enabled on the appliance itself each time, by pressing the Remote button after loading and closing the door. Remote start switches off automatically when the door is opened.

## Software updates
Appliances download updates automatically overnight when they are idle. An update never starts during a programme. The current software version is shown under Settings, then About.

## Data and privacy
The app stores cycle history, error codes and energy readings. It does not collect audio, video or location data. You can download or delete your data at any time from Settings, then Privacy. Deleting your account removes all appliance data within 30 days.

## Voice assistants
Connected appliances work with Amazon Alexa and Google Home for status questions and for starting or pausing a cycle. Apple HomeKit is not supported.
