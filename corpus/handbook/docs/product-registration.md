# Product Registration

## Why register
Registering your appliance links the serial number to your account. It lets us contact you about safety notices, makes warranty claims faster because proof of purchase is already on file, and adds 3 months to the standard warranty when you register within 30 days of delivery.

## How to register
Register online at halden.example/register or scan the QR code on the inside of the appliance door with the Halden Home app. You need the model number, the serial number and the delivery date. Registration takes about two minutes.

## Finding the serial number
- Dishwashers: on the rating plate on the inner edge of the door, right hand side.
- Washing machines and dryers: inside the door opening, below the drum.
- Refrigerators: on the left wall of the fresh food compartment, behind the top crisper drawer.

The serial number is 12 characters long and starts with two letters that identify the product family, for example DW for dishwashers.

## Registering a gift or second hand appliance
You can register an appliance you did not buy yourself. Enter the original delivery date if you know it. If you do not, we use the manufacturing date encoded in the serial number.

## Updating your details
If you move house, update your address in your account so that safety notices reach you. Registration details are never shared with third parties for marketing.
