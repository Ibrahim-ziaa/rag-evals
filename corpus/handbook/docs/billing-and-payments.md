# Billing and Payments

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and bank transfer. We do not accept cheques or cash on delivery. Orders paid by bank transfer are dispatched once the funds have cleared, which usually takes 2 business days.

## When you are charged
Card payments are authorised when you place the order and captured when the order is dispatched. Back ordered items are charged separately when each item is dispatched.

## Refund timing
Once a refund has been approved, it is sent to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear on your statement. PayPal refunds arrive within 24 hours. Refunds for orders paid by bank transfer take up to 10 business days, and we will ask you to confirm your account details.

## Invoices and receipts
An invoice is emailed when the order is dispatched. You can download a copy of any invoice from the Orders page for 7 years. If you need a company name or tax number added to an invoice, contact support within 30 days of the invoice date.

## Sales tax
Sales tax is calculated at checkout from the delivery address. Tax exempt organisations can upload an exemption certificate in their account settings before ordering. We cannot remove tax from an order that has already been dispatched.

## Failed payments
If a card payment fails at dispatch we email you and hold the order for 7 days. If payment is not completed within 7 days, the order is cancelled automatically.
