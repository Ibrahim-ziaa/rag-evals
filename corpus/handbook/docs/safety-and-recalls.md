# Safety Information and Recalls

## General safety
Appliances must be connected to an earthed socket. Never use an extension lead or a multi socket adaptor with a major appliance. Children under 8 should not operate the appliance and should be kept away from an open dishwasher door, because knives and detergent are within reach.

## Dryer fire safety
Do not tumble dry items that have been soiled with cooking oil, petrol or solvents, even after washing, because they can ignite. Never stop a dryer before the end of the cool down phase unless you remove the laundry straight away and spread it out.

## Burning smell or water leak
If you smell burning, unplug the appliance and do not use it again until it has been inspected. If an appliance is leaking, close the water shut off valve first and then switch off the power at the circuit breaker, not at the appliance.

## Current recall
Halden has one active recall. DW-400 series dishwashers manufactured between March 2023 and August 2023 may have a faulty door latch that lets the door open during a cycle. Affected serial numbers begin with DW23 followed by a number between 0300000 and 0899999. The repair is free, takes about 30 minutes and is carried out in your home.

## How to check whether you are affected
Enter your serial number at halden.example/recall or call the recall line on 1-800-555-0142. Stop using an affected dishwasher until the latch has been replaced. Registered owners have already been contacted by email and post.

## Disposal of old appliances
Refrigerators contain refrigerant gas that must be recovered by a licensed handler. Do not cut the pipes or leave an old refrigerator where children could climb inside.
