# Installing Your Washing Machine

## Remove the transit bolts
Four transit bolts hold the drum in place during shipping. Remove all four with the spanner supplied before using the machine, and cover the holes with the plastic caps. Running the machine with the transit bolts in place causes violent shaking and damage that is not covered by the warranty. Keep the bolts, because you will need them if you move house.

## Position and levelling
Place the machine on a firm, flat floor. Suspended wooden floors may need a reinforcing board. Adjust the four feet until the machine does not rock, then tighten the lock nuts against the body. A machine that is not level will be noisy during the spin cycle.

## Water and drain
Connect the grey inlet hose to a cold water tap with a three quarter inch thread. W-series machines heat their own water and have no hot water inlet. Hook the drain hose over a standpipe between 600 mm and 1,000 mm above the floor. Do not push the drain hose more than 150 mm into the standpipe, because this can cause siphoning.

## Power
Plug the machine into an earthed socket that remains accessible after installation. W-series machines draw up to 2,200 watts and should not share a socket with a dryer.

## First wash
Run the Cotton 60 programme with no laundry and a small amount of detergent. This clears any water left over from factory testing.

## Stacking a dryer
A Halden dryer can be stacked on a W-series washing machine only with the SK-2 stacking kit. Never stack a washing machine on top of a dryer.
