# Energy and Water Use

## Eco programmes
The Eco programme is the most efficient way to wash a normally soiled load. It uses lower temperatures and a longer soak, which is why it takes longer than other programmes. On D-series dishwashers the Eco 50 programme runs for 3 hours 40 minutes and uses 9.5 litres of water.

## Typical consumption
- D-series dishwasher, Eco 50: 0.74 kWh and 9.5 litres per cycle.
- W-series washing machine, Eco 40-60 at full load: 0.62 kWh and 48 litres per cycle.
- Heat pump dryer, Cotton cupboard dry at full load: 1.4 kWh per cycle.
- R-series 400 litre refrigerator: 198 kWh per year.

## Ways to use less
Run dishwashers and washing machines with full loads, because a half load uses far more than half the energy. Wash at 30 degrees when clothes are lightly soiled. Spin laundry at the highest speed the fabric allows before using the dryer. Keep the refrigerator at least two thirds full, because food holds the cold better than air does.

## Delay start and off peak tariffs
All Halden appliances have a delay start of up to 24 hours, so a cycle can run when electricity is cheaper. The Halden Home app can schedule cycles around your tariff if you enter your off peak hours.

## Standby power
Appliances use less than 0.5 watts in standby. Connected appliances with Wi-Fi switched on use about 2 watts in standby.
