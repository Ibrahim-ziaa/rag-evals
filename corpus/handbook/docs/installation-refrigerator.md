# Installing Your Refrigerator

## Let it stand
After delivery, leave the refrigerator standing upright and unplugged for at least 4 hours before switching it on. If it was transported lying on its side, wait 24 hours. This allows the compressor oil to settle.

## Clearances
Leave a gap of 50 mm behind the refrigerator, 25 mm above it and 10 mm at each side so that air can circulate. If the door opens against a wall, leave 65 mm on the hinge side so the door can open far enough to remove the drawers. Do not install the refrigerator next to an oven or in direct sunlight.

## Ambient temperature
R-series refrigerators are designed for rooms between 10 and 38 degrees Celsius. They are not suitable for an unheated garage, because in a cold room the freezer may stop cooling.

## Water line for ice and water models
Models with an ice maker need a cold water line with a quarter inch compression fitting. Flush 4 litres of water through the dispenser after connecting the line, and discard the first two batches of ice.

## Reversing the door
The doors on single door models can be re-hung to open from the other side. The hinge kit is in the bag with the manual, and the job takes about 30 minutes with a screwdriver and a 10 mm socket. French door models cannot be reversed.

## Setting the temperature
We recommend 4 degrees Celsius for the fresh food compartment and minus 18 degrees Celsius for the freezer. The refrigerator takes about 12 hours to reach temperature after it is first switched on. Wait until then before loading it fully.
