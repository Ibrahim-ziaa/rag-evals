# Haul Away and Recycling

## Haul away service
When we deliver a new appliance we can take your old one away for recycling. Haul away costs 30 USD per appliance and must be added to the order before the delivery day. The driver cannot accept an appliance that was not booked.

## Preparing the old appliance
The old appliance must be disconnected from water, power and gas, emptied, and moved to the same room where the new one is going. Refrigerators and freezers must be defrosted and dry. For safety reasons our delivery teams do not disconnect gas appliances.

## What happens to it
Old appliances go to a certified recycling partner. On average 85 percent of a Halden appliance by weight is recovered as steel, copper, aluminium and plastic. Refrigerant gas is captured and destroyed by a licensed handler.

## Packaging
Room of choice and installation deliveries include removal of all packaging. With standard delivery the packaging stays with you. The cardboard and the moulded pulp corner blocks can go in household recycling. The plastic film is LDPE and is accepted at most supermarket collection points.

## Recycling without a new purchase
If you are not buying a new appliance, many local councils collect large appliances for free. Halden also runs drop off days at its regional depots twice a year, which are listed at halden.example/recycle.
