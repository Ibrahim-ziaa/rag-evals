# Warranty Policy

## Coverage periods
Every Halden dishwasher, washing machine, dryer and refrigerator carries a limited warranty of 24 months from the delivery date. The sealed refrigeration system on refrigerators (compressor, condenser and evaporator) is covered for 10 years. The direct drive motor on W-series washing machines is also covered for 10 years. Accessories such as racks, shelves and hoses carry a 12 month warranty.

## What is covered
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use. Halden repairs the appliance at no charge for parts or labour. If the same fault cannot be fixed after two repair attempts, we replace the appliance with the same or an equivalent model.

## What is not covered
- Damage caused by incorrect installation, including flooding from a hose that was not tightened.
- Commercial use, including shared laundry rooms and short term rental properties.
- Cosmetic damage such as dents and scratches reported more than 14 days after delivery.
- Consumable parts: water filters, light bulbs and door gaskets worn by normal use.
- Damage from power surges, frozen pipes or pests.

## How to make a claim
Contact support with the serial number and proof of purchase. The serial number is printed on the rating plate inside the door. In most regions a technician visit is booked within 5 business days. You do not need to have registered the product to make a claim, although registration makes the process faster.

## Transfer of ownership
The warranty follows the appliance, not the buyer. If you sell or give away the appliance, the remaining warranty passes to the new owner automatically and no paperwork is needed.
