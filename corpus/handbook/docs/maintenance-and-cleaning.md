# Maintenance and Cleaning

## Dishwasher
Clean the filter every two weeks by twisting it out, rinsing it under the tap and brushing away grease. Check the spray arm holes every three months and clear them with a toothpick. Refill the salt container when the salt indicator lights up.

## Washing machine
Clean the drain pump filter every 3 months. Place a shallow tray under the flap, because up to a litre of water will run out. Wipe the door seal weekly and run the Drum Clean programme monthly. Descale only if your water is very hard, using a descaler made for washing machines.

## Dryer
Clean the lint filter after every load. A blocked lint filter makes drying times longer and is a fire risk. On heat pump dryers, rinse the condenser filter under the tap once a month and let it dry fully before refitting it.

## Refrigerator
Vacuum the condenser coils behind the lower rear panel once a year. Dust on the coils makes the compressor work harder and increases energy use. Replace the water filter every 6 months. Clean the door gaskets with warm soapy water and avoid bleach, which makes the rubber brittle.

## Stainless steel surfaces
Wipe stainless steel in the direction of the grain with a microfibre cloth and a little washing up liquid. Do not use scouring pads, chlorine cleaners or glass cleaner that contains ammonia.

## Going away
If the refrigerator will be switched off for more than 4 weeks, empty it, clean it and leave the doors propped open to prevent mould.
