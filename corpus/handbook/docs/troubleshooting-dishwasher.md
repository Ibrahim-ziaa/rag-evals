# Dishwasher Troubleshooting

## Dishes are not clean
Check that the spray arms spin freely and that nothing tall blocks them. Clean the filter at the bottom of the tub, because a blocked filter is the most common cause of poor wash results. Do not pre-rinse dishes under the tap: the sensor then measures clean water and shortens the wash.

## Dishes are still wet at the end of the cycle
Plastic items hold less heat than china and dry poorly in any dishwasher. Make sure the rinse aid dispenser is full, because rinse aid is what makes water run off the dishes. Select the Extra Dry option, and open the door slightly when the programme ends to let steam escape.

## Water is left in the bottom
A small amount of water in the filter well is normal. If water covers the base of the tub, check the drain hose for kinks and clean the filter. If the sink has a new waste disposal unit, make sure the knockout plug in the disposal inlet was removed.

## White film or spots on glasses
White film is caused by hard water. Fill the salt container and raise the water softener setting. Cloudiness that does not wipe off is etching from soft water and too much detergent, so use less detergent.

## The dishwasher will not start
Check that the door is fully latched and that Child Lock is off, which is shown by a key symbol on the display. Hold the Start button for 3 seconds to cancel any delayed start. If the display is blank, check the fuse or circuit breaker.

## Bad smell
Run the Machine Care programme at 70 degrees with a dishwasher cleaner once a month, and wipe the door gasket.
