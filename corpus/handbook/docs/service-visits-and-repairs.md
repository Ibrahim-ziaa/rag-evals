# Service Visits and Repairs

## Booking a technician
Book a visit from the Support page of your account, in the Halden Home app or by phone. You choose a morning slot (8 am to 12 pm) or an afternoon slot (12 pm to 5 pm), and the technician calls 30 minutes before arriving. An adult must be at home for the whole visit.

## What a visit costs
Visits for a fault covered by the warranty or by Halden Care are free. Outside warranty, the call out fee is 95 USD, which includes the diagnosis and the first 30 minutes of labour. Further labour is charged at 60 USD per half hour, plus parts. You receive a written quote before any chargeable work begins.

## No fault found
If the technician finds no fault with the appliance, or finds that the problem was caused by installation or by the household water or power supply, a 95 USD call out fee applies even during the warranty period.

## Rescheduling and cancelling
You can reschedule or cancel a visit free of charge up to 24 hours before the slot. Later cancellations and missed appointments are charged 40 USD.

## Repair guarantee
All repairs are guaranteed for 12 months, covering both the parts fitted and the labour. If the same fault returns within that period, we repair it again at no cost.

## When a repair is not economical
If the repair quote is more than 60 percent of the price of an equivalent new appliance, we offer a 20 percent loyalty discount on a replacement instead, and the call out fee is refunded.
